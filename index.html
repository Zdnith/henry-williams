Overview:

Henry Williams was born 11/02/1792 and died on 16/07/1867. He was an influential missionary, who came to Aotearoa, New Zealand to take charge of the CMS (Church Missionary Society) mission.

Specific Details:

- He joined CMS in 1819 as a lay settler.
- Became an actual missionary candidate in 1820.
- He was then ordained as a priest in 1822.
- In 1823, he took charge of the CMS mission, allowing him to make great impacts to the spread of Christianity in NZ.[See Impacts page]
- Arrived on the Bay of Islands on August 3, when he took charge.
- He revived schooling Māori with his wife.
- Was resolving Māori conflicts by the late 1820s.
- Translated the Treaty of Waitangi which incorrect meanings, which caused major problems that leads into the modern day.
- Controversies (such as land purchase disputes) ended up with Williams being dismissed from CMS in 1849.
- He ended up being reinstated in 1854.
